## Stage 4 (9 November 2026): C5, amounts with commas

In **Field settings**, under the Field type buttons, shown only when the field is an **Amount**: a tick box, off unless ticked.

- New: `Print with commas and 2 decimals (47752.9 prints as 47,752.90)`

What it does (no text of its own): only what is printed changes, on the preview and in the PDF. What was typed or imported is kept as it was. Nothing is rounded: a value with more than two decimals, or that is not clearly one number, prints exactly as typed. Every template saved before prints as it did.
